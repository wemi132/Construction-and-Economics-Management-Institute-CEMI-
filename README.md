# Construction-and-Economics-Management-Institute-CEMI

**A Digital Institutional Platform | Website, CMS & Learning Management System**

🌐 **Live Platform:** https://cemi.mglobal.co.za

## Overview

CEMI (Construction and Economics Management Institute) is a digital institutional platform designed to establish an online presence and support academic and administrative operations.

The platform brings together institutional information, course management, student services and digital learning resources in one centralized environment.

## Key Features

- **Institutional Website:** Responsive website presenting institutional information and services.
- **Content Management System (CMS):** Management of institutional content and information.
- **Learning Management System (LMS):** Digital learning and course-related functionality.
- **Student Management:** Student enrolment and academic information workflows.
- **Course Management:** Organisation and presentation of available courses.
- **Digital Learning Resources:** Access to online educational materials.
- **Payment Integration:** Online payment functionality for institutional services.
